Omnissiah hero models

Monsters (goblin, goblin-shaman, orc-warrior, troll, dark-knight, demon) are modified derivatives of the KayKit Adventurers characters by Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.com), released CC0 (public domain).
Dragon, dragon-whelp, the Omnissiah regalia (omni-*) and the signature weapons were modelled from scratch for this game and are released CC0 as well.
