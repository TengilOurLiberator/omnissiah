OMNISSIAH

The second sun is waking

Controls

Talk hold T and speak · tap N to talk until you stop (tap again cancels) · or just say “Omnissiah, …” · or type below and press Enter.

Move WASD · click the view, then the mouse looks around · Shift sprints · Space or Q jumps.

Cast left mouse · E spell wheel · G or right mouse grabs.

Menu M opens it · Esc closes it · V enters VR.

In VR hold the LEFT trigger to talk, tap X to talk until you stop, or say “Omnissiah, …” · RIGHT trigger casts · A spell wheel · B jumps · grips grab · left stick moves, right stick turns · left B opens the menu.
